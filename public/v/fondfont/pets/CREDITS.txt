Adaptations by lazyapps: conversion from Blender 2.77 to a real-time GLB;
two cyclic rig constraints removed in the derived copy; original native IK
adapted for grounded walking, turning and planted-paw corrections; original
combed parent hair converted into simplified 3D strands bound to evaluated
skin triangle frames; original eye color graph baked; texture compression.
Original body painting, animal surfaces and source groom are retained.
Two instances share the original geometry and textures. The second instance
uses a charcoal coat shader; its eyes and the original instance are unchanged.
The web animation is an adaptation, not the unmodified authored Walk action.
