## About the app

Offline world atlas for iPhone and iPad, with country profiles, a 3D globe and rankings.

Browse geography, history, people and economy, and compare countries using interactive charts with reported values, dates and ranks. Profiles, flags, rankings, locator maps and the globe work offline. Photos and regional maps require an initial download. Choose from four reading themes. One-time purchase; no in-app purchases, subscriptions, ads or tracking. World Book reads archived CIA World Factbook data; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CIA. Data is not live, and figures may refer to different years.
