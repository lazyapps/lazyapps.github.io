## About the app

Install your TTF, OTF and TTC files in the iOS font library. Import ZIP collections, finish installation in Settings and use your fonts in compatible apps such as Pages and Keynote.

Install your TTF, OTF and TTC files in the iOS font library. Import ZIP collections, finish installation in Settings and use your fonts in compatible apps such as Pages and Keynote.

Check your own text across different point sizes.

Inspect character coverage, family, style and license information.

Compare typefaces using the same sample text.

Open your TTF, OTF, TTC, or ZIP files from where you already keep them.

FondFont creates a profile on your device. Allow its download, then finish installation in Settings.

Choose your installed fonts in compatible apps such as Pages, Keynote, Word and PowerPoint.

FondFont creates profiles on your device. Your font files stay on your device. You do not need an account or subscription.

FondFont installs fonts for compatible apps. It cannot change the iOS system font, keyboard font or Home Screen font. It cannot add font support to other apps.
