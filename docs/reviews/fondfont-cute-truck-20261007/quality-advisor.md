The minimum meaningful change is to replace the cab’s modeling method, not add detail. An extruded side profile with beveled edges cannot produce the coordinated curvature of the front, windshield, pillars, shoulders, and roof. More accessories will preserve that mismatch.

For this hero, rebuild the cab as an editable subdivision surface in Blender; retain the chassis, bed, gate, rig contract, and choreography. Use a small, deliberate quad cage, with sections controlling width and curvature at the bumper, beltline, windshield base, and roof. Establish the complete outer shell before cutting windows or separating doors. Fit glass and trim to that shell.

**A procedural loft can generate the starting cage. It should not be treated as the finished automotive surface.** The workflow must permit direct control-point editing and render-led correction. Replacing one automatic geometry recipe with another, followed by bevels and decorative details, risks the same result.

I would not make finding a free, exact-match professional truck a dependency. A licensed cab-over donor could accelerate this, but only after inspecting its topology, proportions, materials, and redistribution rights. If direct surface modeling is unavailable, acquiring such a donor is preferable to another scripted refinement.

Use these visual gates, in order:

1. **Silhouette:** camera-match the reference’s three-quarter view. In a thumbnail clay render, cab stance, windshield rake, roof curvature, wheel diameter, and bed proportions must read convincingly. Stop if they do not.
2. **Surface quality:** under broad studio reflections, inspect the front corners, pillars, roof transitions, and window surrounds. Reject pinching, faceting, inflated bevels, and slab-like glass.
3. **Materials and wheels:** test fresh neutral red paint, rubber, glass, and metal without inherited diffuse textures. Refine tire shoulders and wheel depth before tread or bolts.
4. **Actual hero:** approve at browser display size, then inspect the animation’s most revealing angles. Require explicit visual approval before adding microdetail.
