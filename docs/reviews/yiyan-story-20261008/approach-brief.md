User: “视屏不要做成了说明书，换个方向”. They already rejected a verbose 25s film; our current 15s film is still a feature list: refinement, notes, recall, handoff. They want a different creative direction. Original constraint: use local MiniMax H3; advisor invoked. No need to change the newly concise website feature inventory. No publication.

Product: YiYan turns everyday input into natural English and focused learning material, passively capturing new AI-tool prompts or accepting direct/focused-field input. Distinctive promise: use your own words as practice instead of extra lessons. Local default, real AI still used; no guaranteed fluency and no magical offline generation.

Proposed 12s lifestyle mini-film, silent but expressive:
- 0–4s: candid afternoon workspace, close over-shoulder view of a young adult East Asian woman in an understated navy overshirt typing on a plain silver laptop. Face and readable UI excluded; hands/shoulder/laptop context visible. One editorial quote (not fake UI): “我们明天接着聊。” Warm natural sunlight. Caption minimal or omitted.
- 4–8s: next day, same woman and wardrobe at a neighborhood café table with another adult friend, relaxed real conversation; gesture and slight smile, camera excludes mouth closeups and avoids lip-sync claims. The same-position editorial quote becomes “Let’s pick this up tomorrow.” Communicates using a familiar phrase in life, not teaching steps. Do not show classroom, phone screen, feature cards, app screenshots or paragraph labels.
- 8–12s: clean ivory end card, authentic YiYan icon and brand, one line: “把说过的话，变成自己的英语。” + lazyapps.com/yiyan. No feature list, no install tutorial, no standalone title at beginning.

Two photoreal first-frame anchors generated with built-in imagegen; animate only using local MiniMax H3. Target natural cinematic everyday realism, restrained palette and match cut of hands/gesture; no holograms, flying letters, glowing UI or stock corporate team. Four seconds per live shot (genuine 3s H3 clip slowed modestly), four-second brand payoff. All 12 localized end cards; Chinese source quote and English usable phrase intentionally retained as the narrative sample across languages. The quote is editorial, not presented as an authentic recorded app result.
