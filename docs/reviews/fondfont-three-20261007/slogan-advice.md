- **en**\
  We don’t make fonts.\
  We just deliver yours.

- **ja**\
  フォントはつくりません。\
  あなたのフォントを届けます。\
  Use 届けます: more natural for delivery to a destination than 運びます, while preserving the metaphor.

- **ko**\
  폰트는 만들지 않아요.\
  여러분의 폰트를 옮겨 드려요.\
  옮겨 드려요 sounds warmer and more helpful than 옮길 뿐이죠.

- **fr**\
  On ne crée pas de polices.\
  On livre les vôtres.\
  Livre gives the line a concise delivery-service feel; transporte sounds more logistical.

- **de**\
  Wir gestalten keine Schriften.\
  Wir bringen deine ans Ziel.

**Ownership ambiguity:** All five possessive expressions can suggest fonts the user designed, rather than font files they already have. The supporting paragraph should explicitly establish the latter meaning. The foundry animation alone does not resolve this and could suggest FondFont supplies the fonts.
